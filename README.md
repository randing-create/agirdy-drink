# Which drink are you? 🍸

A small phone game for **2x40+1**, the party for one year of AGIRDY and 40 years of Philipp and Jan, on 17 October 2026 at Maschinenhalle Süd, Motorworld Zeche Ewald in Herten.

**Play it here: https://randing-create.github.io/agirdy-drink/**

No app, no sign-up. Scan the QR code and you're in. English and German.

## How it works

1. **Find your ingredient.** Eight quick questions tell you which part of a cocktail you are: Citrus, Spirit, Syrup or Fizz.
2. **Pick your areas.** On the Wheel of Life, choose up to three areas where AI could help you most. Each one comes with a concrete idea of what AI could do there.
3. **Mix your team.** Find two people with a different coaster color. Together you make a real drink, shown in its own glass and color.
4. **Meet in the middle.** Compare your cards, find where your wheels meet and write one idea for something AI could build. The favorite ideas get built live on stage during the evening.

## The four ingredients

| Ingredient | At the bar | In a team |
|---|---|---|
| 🟡 Citrus | lemon, lime, grapefruit, orange | tests the idea |
| 🟠 Spirit | gin, rum, tequila, whisky, vodka | gets it done |
| 🔴 Syrup | sugar syrup, honey, agave, grenadine | keeps people in mind |
| 🔵 Fizz | soda, tonic, ginger beer, prosecco | brings the spark |

## The twelve drinks

| Mix | Drink | Glass | Recipe |
|---|---|---|---|
| Citrus + Spirit | Gimlet | Coupe | gin, lime cordial |
| Citrus + Syrup | Lemonade | Highball | lemon, sugar syrup, water (alcohol-free) |
| Citrus + Fizz | Lime & Soda | Highball | fresh lime, soda (alcohol-free) |
| Spirit + Syrup | Old Fashioned *or* Espresso Martini | Rocks / Coupe | whisky, sugar, bitters / vodka, coffee liqueur, espresso, sugar syrup |
| Spirit + Fizz | Gin & Tonic | Balloon | gin, tonic water |
| Syrup + Fizz | Kir Royal | Flute | crème de cassis, champagne |
| Citrus + Spirit + Syrup | Margarita | Coupe | tequila, lime, agave syrup |
| Citrus + Spirit + Fizz | Paloma | Highball | tequila, grapefruit, lime, soda |
| Citrus + Syrup + Fizz | Virgin Mojito | Highball | lime, mint, sugar syrup, soda (alcohol-free) |
| Spirit + Syrup + Fizz | Aperol Spritz *or* Osmanthus Oolong 桂花乌龙 | Wine glass / Highball | Aperol, prosecco, soda / gin or baijiu, cold oolong tea, osmanthus syrup, soda |

## Under the hood

- One self-contained `index.html`. No build step, no framework, no tracking, no data stored.
- Fonts live in this repository, so the page makes no requests to Google or any other third party.
- Respects dark and light mode and reduced-motion settings.
